page 1 / for you

For you

hey, you.

I know we had an argument.

And honestly, I hate that the person I want to make smile the most is sometimes the person I end up upsetting.

So before anything else...

I'm sorry. ♥

Not the casual "sorry, okay?" kind.

I mean the "I wish I could go back, hug you, and handle things differently" kind.

I don't want to win arguments with you.

I want to win you.

Because at the end of the day, it's never you vs me.

It's always us vs the problem.

And sometimes I may be stubborn, stupid, dramatic, or all three at once...

...but one thing I'm never confused about:

I love you.

So this little app is my very dramatic, slightly cheesy, completely genuine way of saying:

Come back to smiling at me?

P.S. You are still my favorite notification. Even when you're angry. Okay... I do get scared.

page 3 / the apology

okay... my turn to be serious.

I know saying sorry doesn't magically erase an argument.

And I'm not going to make excuses.

If something I said or did hurt you, I'm genuinely sorry.

I never want you to feel like your feelings don't matter to me.

Because they do. You do.

Sometimes emotions get louder than logic. Sometimes we misunderstand each other. Sometimes two people who love each other can still hurt each other.

But I don't want one bad moment to become bigger than everything beautiful we've built together.

I'm sorry for the argument.

I'm sorry for the things I could have handled better.

I'm sorry if I made you feel unheard, upset, or unloved.

I'm sorry for forgetting, even for a moment, that you're not my enemy.

You're my person. The one I want beside me. The one I want to laugh with. The one I want to annoy for the rest of my life.

"Hey idiot, we're supposed to be on the same team."

Don't let one bad day make you forget all our good ones. Because I haven't forgotten them. Not even one.

page 4 / one last thing

i don't want perfect.

I don't expect us to never argue. We're two different people. We'll disagree, get annoyed, and misunderstand each other sometimes.

Maybe I'll occasionally say something stupid...

Actually, let's be realistic. I definitely will.

But I don't want a relationship where we never fight. I want one where, after the fight, we still choose each other.

so here's my promise. ♥

I'll try to listen better.

I'll try to understand before reacting.

I'll try to be more careful with your heart.

And whenever things get difficult, I'll remember that you are more important than being right.
